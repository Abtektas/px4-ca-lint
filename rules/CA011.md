# CA011: thrust command to an actuator that produces no thrust on that axis

Level: warning

## What it checks

An actuator has a mix entry above 0.05 on a thrust axis although its
effectiveness on that axis is zero.

## Why it matters

The setpoint on that thrust axis changes the output of an actuator that cannot
contribute to it. The allocation uses the actuator to cancel a torque that
another actuator produces. On a standard VTOL this means that forward thrust
changes the commands of the lift motors.

## Example

`examples/standard_vtol_pusher_offset.params`: the pusher above the centre of
gravity produces a pitch torque, and the four lift motors get a thrust_x entry
of 0.25 to cancel it.
