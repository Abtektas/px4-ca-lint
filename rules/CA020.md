# CA020: a motor is not used for roll or pitch

Level: warning

## What it checks

A motor produces both roll and pitch torque according to the effectiveness
matrix, but its mix entries for roll and for pitch are both zero.

## Why it matters

On a symmetric multirotor every motor away from the centre takes part in roll
or pitch control. A motor that is left out usually means that the torques of
the motors do not balance the way the geometry suggests.

## Example

`examples/quad_wrong_spin.params`: a quadrotor where one motor has the wrong
sign of `CA_ROTOR3_KM`, which is the spin direction. The mix does not use
motor2 for roll or pitch, and the thrust_z entries of the motors become
unequal.

## What to do

Check the spin directions (`CA_ROTORn_KM`: positive is counter-clockwise,
negative is clockwise) and the rotor positions. The motor named in the finding
is not always the one with the wrong parameter.
