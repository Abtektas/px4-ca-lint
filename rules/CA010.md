# CA010: thrust gain above the limit

Level: warning

## What it checks

In the mix matrix, the entry of an actuator on a thrust axis (thrust_x,
thrust_y, thrust_z) is larger than the limit. The default limit is 2.0 and can
be changed with `--max-thrust-gain`.

## Why it matters

PX4 normalises the thrust columns of the mix matrix, so a thrust setpoint of 1
normally gives an actuator command of about 1. With a gain of 4, the actuator
reaches full output at a setpoint of 0.25 and everything above is cut off.

## Example

`examples/standard_vtol_pusher_offset.params`: a standard VTOL whose pusher
motor is 5 cm above the centre of gravity (`CA_ROTOR4_PZ -0.05`). The pusher
gets a thrust_x gain of 4.0 instead of 1.0
([PX4 issue 28829](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot/issues/28829)).

## Limits

The default limit was chosen so that none of the multirotor and standard VTOL
airframe scripts shipped with PX4 v1.17.0 triggers the rule. PX4's omnicopter
has gains of 1.66.
