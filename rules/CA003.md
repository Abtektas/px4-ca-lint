# CA003: a matrix contains NaN or infinity

Level: error

## What it checks

The effectiveness matrix or the mix matrix contains a value that is not a
finite number.

## Why it matters

Actuator commands computed from such a matrix are not defined. The other rules
are not run on that matrix, because their results would not mean anything.

## What to do

Look for parameters with extreme values, for example a rotor axis
(`CA_ROTORn_AX`, `_AY`, `_AZ`) that is all zero, or a thrust coefficient
`CA_ROTORn_CT` of zero.
